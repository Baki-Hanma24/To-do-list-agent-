# Content playbook: what works for me

Built from my best-performing reel. It's one reel, so treat this as a strong signal, not a law. Update it every time a new reel does well (use `templates/reel-breakdown.md`).

## Best reel so far: "hard can it be…" (the high-level dead bug)

**Results:** 146,255 views · 121,388 viewers · 4.1K likes · 6.2K saves · 1.6K shares · 63 reposts · 27 comments · 84 follows · avg watch 17s of ~46s. A collab with hanma_academy, crossposted to Facebook.

| Signal | Rate | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Saves | ~4.2% of views | Excellent. More saves than likes means people kept it to try later. |
| Shares | ~1.1% of views | Strong. People sent it to training partners, which drove reach. |
| Watch time | ~37% (17s / 46s) | The hook pulled people in, then the long set lost some. |
| Follows | ~0.07% of viewers | Weak. Huge reach, little conversion. The biggest opportunity. |
| Comments | 27 | Low. Nothing asked for a reply. |

**Caption:** "hard can it be…"

**Structure:**
1. **Hook (0-3s):** Squat University's viral "THIS IS A HIGH LEVEL… DEAD BUG" reel plays in the background while I say, "I don't know what compelled me to make this video, but let's try it." Then a popular trending sound effect (a guy yelling "f***!") hits over a shot of me visibly wrecked in the aftermath.
2. **Demo:** a cinematic wide shot in a dark, backlit gym. I do it while giving an honest voiceover. **No script.** None of my edits have ever followed one.
3. **Live self-correction:** in the voiceover I realize it's similar to a **pullover** (a lat exercise) and fix my form on camera. The viewer learns the *why* through my own discovery.
4. **Payoff:** the full barbell-overhead set, done right.
5. **Ending:** right as I finish, I say "oh sh*t" in pain, and the video loops straight back to the start.

**Why it worked:**
- **Relatable humility:** "let's try it" plus being visibly humbled. The viewer is on my side.
- **Trending sound as a pattern interrupt:** a laugh in the first seconds stops the scroll.
- **Social proof:** the original reel was already viral (about 156K likes), so the challenge was pre-tested.
- **Authenticity:** unscripted, real thoughts, real effort. People trust it.
- **Learning in public:** the pullover connection is a non-obvious insight, a big reason people saved it.
- **Funny, honest ending that loops:** the "oh sh*t" in pain bookends the yell in the hook, and the instant restart makes people rewatch. Rewatches count toward watch time and tell the algorithm to push it.
- **Understated, ironic caption:** "hard can it be…" invites curiosity.
- **Save- and share-worthy:** an advanced core movement people want to try with a partner.
- **Cinematic look:** dark gym, strong backlight, a small figure in a big space. It stands out from phone-camera clips.
- **Collab:** the hanma_academy collab reached a second audience.

**What to fix next time:**
- **Follows:** tell people why to follow. Make it a series ("Trying viral exercises, part 2 on my page"). Put it in on-screen text or the caption, not a spoken outro, so the loop stays clean.
- **Comments:** ask one easy question ("What should I try next?") or use a keyword.
- **Watch time:** put the realization on screen at ~10-15s (e.g. "It's basically a pullover"), and trim the long set.
- **Credit:** tag the original creators (@squat_university, @luxiaojunsport, athlete @lidayin89kg) when using their footage.

## Proven formula (check every reel against this)
- [ ] **Hook in 3s:** a challenge or proof that it's hard, plus a trending sound or pattern interrupt.
- [ ] **A real moment:** struggle, mistake, or realization. Don't hide it.
- [ ] **Unscripted:** talking points, not a script. My real voice.
- [ ] **The why:** one insight on mechanics or anatomy, ideally discovered live.
- [ ] **Save trigger:** something worth trying later.
- [ ] **Share trigger:** "send this to your gym partner" energy.
- [ ] **Follow trigger:** a series or next part.
- [ ] **Comment trigger:** one easy question or keyword.
- [ ] **Loop ending:** end on a raw, funny, or honest beat that cuts straight back to the start. No outro.
- [ ] **Cinematic look:** dark, moody gym, strong light source, wide angles.
- [ ] **Credit** any borrowed footage.
- [ ] **Tight:** 30-45s, no dead stretches.
