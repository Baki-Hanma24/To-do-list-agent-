# Baki deep dive

## The series

- **Creator:** Keisuke Itagaki. **Publisher:** Akita Shoten, serialized in *Weekly Shōnen Champion*.
- **Main run, in order:**

  | Title | Years | Volumes |
  |---|---|---|
  | *Grappler Baki* | 1991–1999 | 42 |
  | *Baki* | 1999–2005 | 31 |
  | *Baki Hanma* (*Hanma Baki*) | 2005–2012 | 37 |
  | *Baki-Dou* | 2014–2018 | 22 |
  | *Baki-Dou* part 2 | 2018–2023 | — |
  | *Baki Rahen* | 2023– | ongoing |

- **Side stories:** *Baki Gaiden: GaiA* (2009), *Baki: Revenge Tokyo* (2018), and a 2019 remake of chapter 1.
- **Size:** about 150 volumes in total; sources disagree (149-156). Over 75 million copies sold, with newer figures citing 100 million+ *(the figures vary by source)*.
- **English publishing:** Gutsoon! Entertainment published the early run as *Baki the Grappler*. Media Do licensed it in 2018. Kodama Tales began a worldwide English release in October 2025.

## The anime (most recent first)

- **2024: *Baki Hanma VS Kengan Ashura*** (Netflix, June 6, 2024). A TMS Entertainment crossover film. Director: Toshiki Hirano. Writer: Atsuo Ishino.
- **2023: *Baki Hanma* season 2** on Netflix, released in parts in mid-2023.
- ***Baki Hanma*** (Netflix) adapts the *Hanma Baki* manga ("Son of Ogre").
- **2020: *Baki: The Great Raitai Tournament Saga*** (spring 2020).
- **2018: *Baki*** (Netflix). 26 episodes from TMS Entertainment (some sources also credit Double Eagle). Adapts the *Baki* sequel, starting with the death row convicts arc.
- **Also:** the original 2001 TV series, the 1994 OVA, and *Baki-Dou: The Invincible Samurai* on Netflix (the Musashi arc).

## The creator: Keisuke Itagaki

- **Background:** born in 1957 in Kushiro, Hokkaido *(most sources)*. He served about five years (1976–1981) in the Japan Ground Self-Defense Force's 1st Airborne Brigade, then became a manga artist.
- **His own fighting:** he boxed as an amateur while in the military and competed at the National Sports Festival. He holds a 2nd-degree black belt in **Shorinji Kempo**.
- **Inspiration:** his editor says early UFC fights inspired *Baki*. Itagaki also wanted to show bodybuilder physiques actually fighting and proving their strength.

## What "strength" means in Baki

- **Itagaki's answer:** strength is "the willpower needed to selfishly maintain one's own way" *(English translation, from a 2023 interview)*. He treats it as neutral, neither good nor evil, and it runs through the whole series. His editor adds that strength in Baki means many different things.
- **Discipline over motivation:** gym culture (#GymTok) adopted Baki as an icon because he's a guy who *chooses* to train to his limits. One article calls the power of discipline the central theme of his story.
- **The father:** Yujiro reflects Itagaki's own fantasy of a father figure. Baki's drive to surpass his father is the spine of the series.

## Baki Hanma

- **Who he is:** the son of Yujiro Hanma. His goal is to become strong enough to surpass his father, "the strongest creature on Earth." He fights in an underground arena beneath the Tokyo Dome *(arena owner Mitsunari Tokugawa: unconfirmed in this research)*.
- **Imaginary training ("shadow" sparring):** his signature. He visualizes opponents so vividly they become real enough to fight, most famously a **giant praying mantis of about 100 kg**. He uses a Chinese kenpo mantis style (Tōrōken) to find its weak spot, as preparation for fighting Yujiro. This later pays off in the father-son story.
- **The "demon back":** the Hanma muscle structure that forms a demon's face when flexed. Baki develops it in his fight with his half-brother Jack, but holds it back.
- **The ending of the father-son fight** *(unconfirmed, single fan source)*: Baki forces Yujiro to use his demon back. Yujiro wins the physical exchange, then concedes the title of strongest after Baki's "imaginary miso soup" scene, which echoes the imaginary training.

## Yujiro Hanma, "the Ogre"

- **Who he is:** called "the strongest creature on Earth." His repertoire covers essentially every known fighting style: karate, boxing, **taekwondo**, judo, jujutsu, wrestling, street fighting.
- **His signature:** the demon back, the clearest image of the series.

## The fighters

| Fighter | Style / identity | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Doppo Orochi** | Karate master. Runs the Shinshinkai karate school. | Consistent |
| **Kaoru Hanayama** | Yakuza street fighter. Famous for raw, untrained strength. | Mostly fan sources |
| **Retsu Kaioh** | Chinese martial artist (kenpo), skilled in many styles. | Consistent |
| **Jack Hanma** | Baki's half-brother, a Canadian pit fighter. Used steroids and surgery to grow to Hanma-level power; drugs damaged his body before he rebuilt it. Fights with a biting art called Goudou. | Fan sources |
| **Biscuit Oliva** | A mass of muscle, with a defensive "ball" posture. Said to be based on bodybuilder **Sergio Oliva** (three-time Mr. Olympia, the only man to beat Arnold in a bodybuilding contest). His bulletproof muscles nod to a real incident where Sergio was shot. | Fan sources |
| **Gouki Shibukawa** | Small old master of redirecting force: aikido/jujutsu, "aiki." | Sources disagree on the exact art |
| **Pickle** | A prehistoric man revived in the modern world, pure primal strength. | Thin sources |
| **Musashi Miyamoto** | The legendary swordsman revived (*Baki-Dou*). Two-sword Niten Ichi-ryū, later fighting with "imaginary swords." | Netflix Tudum |

**No core fighter is a Tae Kwon Do specialist** in what I found. The closest link is that taekwondo is part of Yujiro's all-styles repertoire. Kicking-heavy fighters: *(unconfirmed, needs a better source)*.

## Baki and real bodybuilding

- **A gym icon:** Baki is a GymTok and lifting icon. "Train like Baki" articles adapt it sensibly: progressive overload, compound lifts, combat-style conditioning, without the fictional extremes.
- **Real-life look-alike:** Japanese pro bodybuilder **Naotaka Yokokawa** looks so much like Baki that he's done promotional work for the anime.
- **The art itself:** extreme, hyper-detailed muscle anatomy, drawn to make bodybuilder physiques *fight*. That fits Itagaki's stated aim.
