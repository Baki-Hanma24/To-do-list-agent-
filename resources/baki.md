## Baki in brief
- **The series:** a martial arts manga by **Keisuke Itagaki** (1991–present, about 150 volumes). Itagaki served in Japan's airborne brigade, boxed as an amateur, and holds a Shorinji Kempo black belt. Early UFC fights inspired the series.
  - **Manga:** *Grappler Baki* → *Baki* → *Baki Hanma* → *Baki-Dou* → *Baki Rahen*.
  - **Netflix anime:** *Baki* (2018), *Baki Hanma*, and the *Kengan Ashura* crossover (2024).
- **Baki Hanma** trains obsessively to surpass his father. His signature is **imaginary training**: visualizing an opponent, like a 100 kg praying mantis, so vividly that he can fight it.
- **Yujiro Hanma, "the Ogre,"** is "the strongest creature on Earth." He masters every style (taekwondo included), and flexing his back forms the famous **demon back** face.
- **Key fighters:**
  - **Doppo Orochi:** karate master.
  - **Retsu Kaioh:** Chinese kenpo.
  - **Hanayama:** raw strength.
  - **Jack Hanma:** steroids and surgery to grow huge, at a cost.
  - **Biscuit Oliva:** walking muscle, said to be based on Sergio Oliva.
  - **Shibukawa:** redirecting force.
  - **Musashi Miyamoto:** the revived swordsman.
- **What strength means:** Itagaki calls strength the will to stay true to your own way. Gym culture loves Baki for **discipline over motivation**: he chooses to train to his limits.

## Rules for using Baki
- **It's fiction and exaggerated.** Never present Baki training as real advice. Adapt it into evidence-based, safe versions, and say so.
- **No made-up quotes.** Don't invent Baki quotes. Paraphrase themes, or quote only what's verified.
- **Anime footage is copyrighted.** Use short clips sparingly, as a hook or reference, always credited, and prefer my own footage.
- **Not every post.** Baki is part of my identity, not every post. Use it when it fits naturally.
