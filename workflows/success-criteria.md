# Success criteria

A successful output should be:

- **Clear:** easy to scan, with headings and short bullets.
- **Actionable:** says exactly what to do next, with links, prices, and dates.
- **Accurate:** real, current facts. Nothing made up, no placeholders.
- **Concise:** matches the requested length. Never padded.
- **Easy to understand:** plain words, no jargon.
- **Immediately useful:** the user can act on it right now.
