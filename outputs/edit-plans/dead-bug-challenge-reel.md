## 6. Look

The footage already has the look: a dark gym, the open bay door as a strong backlight, and you small in a big space. Only polish it.

- Starting values, then match by eye to the posted reel: contrast +10, highlights −15, shadows −10, saturation −10, warmth −10 (pushes the shadows teal), vignette +15.
- Instagram can't tint only the highlights amber. Keep warmth low and let the warm window light read as the amber.
- **Letterbox:** the original had no bars. Add `public/letterbox-9x16.png` (also downloadable on the app's Editor page) as an image overlay and stretch it to full screen. Check that the bars don't cover the bar path at the top of the rep or your head at the bottom.
